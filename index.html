About

I’m Abhisai, a photographer and Reel maker based in Hyderabad. I photograph the people and places of Telangana as they are: working hands, festival crowds, paddy fields after rain.

Every picture is a memory borrowed from eternity, and I try to bring back the ones that are quietly disappearing.
